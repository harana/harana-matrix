Added `ClientBuilder::search_index_provider`, which searches messages with a
full-text engine of your own instead of the built-in one, and
`SearchIndexStoreKind::Custom`.

Added the `experimental-search-core` feature: the pluggable search plumbing
without a built-in backend. `experimental-search` still means search with the
built-in Tantivy backend and behaves as before.

`Arc<dyn StoreProvider>` now implements `StoreProvider`, so a boxed store
backend can be passed straight to `ClientBuilder::store_provider`.

Re-exported the SQLite store's pluggable cipher and codec traits as
`matrix::store_encryption`, its log targets as
`matrix::sqlite_log_targets`, and `SecretStoreCipherProvider`.
