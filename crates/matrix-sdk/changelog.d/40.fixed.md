`CrossSigningStatus::is_published` now becomes `true` after the cross-signing
identity's public keys reach the homeserver. `bootstrap_cross_signing`,
`reset_cross_signing` and `CrossSigningResetHandle::auth` send
`/keys/device_signing/upload` directly rather than through the outgoing-request
machinery, and never told the `OlmMachine` the upload had succeeded, so the flag
stayed `false` forever and a consumer acting on it would republish an identity
that was already on the server.
