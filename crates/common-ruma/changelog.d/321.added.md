[MSC3834] (opportunistic user key pinning) types, behind the
`unstable-msc3834` feature: the `tofu_signing_key` field on the
`/keys/device_signing/upload` request, the `KeyUsage::TofuSigning` variant, and
the `m.signatures_hash_request` / `m.signatures_hash` to-device events that let
a user's devices check the homeserver is not dropping their cross-signing
signatures. All are under the MSC's `org.matrix.msc3834.v1` unstable prefix,
with the stable names accepted as aliases.
