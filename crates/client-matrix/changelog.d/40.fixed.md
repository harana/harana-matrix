A cross-signing identity that failed to reach the homeserver is published again.
The upload of the cross-signing keys is made by the `Client` rather than by the
`OlmMachine`, and the machine was never told when it succeeded, so the `shared`
flag of the private identity stayed false for a published identity as much as
for a lost one and `CrossSigningStatus::is_published` reported the same thing in
both cases. Every path that uploads the keys now records that the homeserver
accepted them, and `Encryption::bootstrap_cross_signing_if_needed()` publishes
an identity that was never published instead of treating a locally present
identity as the end of the matter.
