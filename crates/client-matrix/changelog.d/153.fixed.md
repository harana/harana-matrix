Logging out now stops the client's background tasks: the send queue is disabled
without respawning room tasks, and the encryption tasks (backup upload and
download, recovery state updates, key bundle receipt, e2ee setup) are aborted.
They previously kept issuing requests to the old homeserver after logout, which
wasted work and went on talking to the previous account's server after a new
login. A sync loop and the services built on the client (sync service, room
list service) own their own tasks and still have to be stopped by their owner.

Logging out also cancels the requests that are still in flight. Their answers
cannot be used for anything: the token they went out with is invalid by then,
and the client may be logged in as somebody else by the time they come back.
Requests made after the logout are unaffected -- this ends a session, it does
not close the client.
