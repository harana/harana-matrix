Expose account registration: `Client::register` drives the user-interactive
authentication flow and reports a typed `RegistrationOutcome`, either
`Registered` or `AuthenticationRequired` carrying the `UiaaChallenge` to
answer, and `Client::request_registration_email_token` asks the homeserver to
validate an email address for the `m.login.email.identity` stage. `AuthData`
gained the stages a registration flow needs: `Dummy`, `RegistrationToken`,
`EmailIdentity` and `FallbackAcknowledgement`. Registration against a
homeserver that authenticates through OAuth 2.0 was already possible with a
`Create` prompt on `Client::url_for_oauth`, per MSC2965.
