Add `Client::change_password`, driving the password change through the
user-interactive authentication flow. It reports a typed
`PasswordChangeOutcome`: either `Changed`, or `AuthenticationRequired` carrying
the `UiaaChallenge` to answer, which is then resubmitted with the matching
`AuthData` and its session. The new password is zeroized once the request has
been sent and never appears in the logs or in the returned value.
