`Logger` now has one method per severity (`error`, `warn`, `info`, `debug`,
`trace`), each taking the log statement's target, its message, and its
structured fields as a map, instead of a single `log` method receiving one
pre-formatted line. Log statements are dispatched through a tracing layer, so
a downstream logger can route by severity and read the fields. Implementations
of `Logger` need updating: replace `log` with the five level methods.
